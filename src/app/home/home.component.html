What does this tool do?

The AI-Powered Solution for Accelerating Defect Resolution (ADR) is designed to automate and streamline defect resolution. By analyzing past Jira issues and resolutions, ADR suggests the best possible solutions for reported defects. It can also automate the entire defect resolution process, from suggesting fixes to applying patches, triggering builds, running tests, and raising change requests (CRs). Once approved, the system proceeds with pull requests (PRs), final validation, and closing JIRA — ensuring faster and more reliable defect resolution.
